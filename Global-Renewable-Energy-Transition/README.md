# Global Renewable Energy Transition (2015–2024)

An interactive data visualization project built using Tableau to explore global renewable energy trends, country-level performance, and investment patterns from 2015 to 2024.

The project focuses on understanding how renewable energy adoption varies across countries and income groups, how investment relates to renewable energy development, and how the global energy transition has evolved over time.

## Project Overview

The dashboard explores global renewable energy data to identify patterns in energy production, installed capacity, investment, and country-level adoption.

The analysis focuses on:
- Global renewable energy trends over time
- Comparison of renewable energy performance across countries
- Renewable energy adoption by income group
- Investment patterns and their relationship with renewable energy development
- Changes in renewable energy production and capacity from 2015 to 2024

## Tableau Dashboard

The interactive dashboard presents multiple views of the global renewable energy transition, allowing users to explore geographic patterns, compare countries, and investigate trends across different dimensions.

### Dashboard Features

- Global renewable energy map
- Country-level performance comparisons
- Renewable energy adoption by income group
- Investment impact analysis
- Renewable energy transition trends over time

## Key Analytical Areas

- **Geographic Analysis:** Explore differences in renewable energy development across countries.
- **Country Comparison:** Identify countries leading in renewable energy production and capacity.
- **Income Group Analysis:** Examine variations in renewable energy adoption across income groups.
- **Investment Analysis:** Explore patterns between investment and renewable energy development.
- **Trend Analysis:** Investigate how renewable energy indicators have changed over the 2015–2024 period.
